0. **What happened to your car?** (`capture-01-picker`): grouped list of cards with icon, title, one-line description. Tapping a card selects it and moves on. Footer note "Your answers save as you go. You can close this page and come back with the same link."
1. **When and where** (`capture-02`): date (required, default today), time (optional), place (optional), city, state (prefilled from the policy, hint "From your policy. Change it if this happened in another state.").
2. **What happened** (adapt `capture-03` style): "Describe what happened in your own words" (textarea, required, min 10 chars), "Was another vehicle involved?" Yes/No chips, "Were the police contacted?" Yes/No/Not yet chips, "Can you drive the car?" Yes/No chips.
3. **Where is the damage?** (`capture-04`): a top-down car outline as inline SVG (front at top, labels "FRONT", "Driver's side (left)", "Passenger side (right)") with tappable zones that highlight amber when selected, plus part chips: Front bumper, Hood, Front left, Front right, Left side, Right side, Rear left, Rear right, Rear bumper, Trunk or tailgate, Roof, Windshield, Rear window, Wheels or tires. Chips and SVG zones stay in sync. Optional textarea "Another part, or anything we missed". At least one part required. Map parts to the claimed area used by the checks: front bumper/hood/front left/front right/windshield → front; rear bumper/trunk/rear left/rear right/rear window → rear; left side → left; right side → right; if parts span several areas use the area with the most selected parts.
4. **Photos** (`capture-05`): "Take each one live with this phone." Session code pill "Session {code} appears on every photo". Progress "N of 4 done". Four cards in this order with number badge, title, one-line hint, "Required" tag, a green "Take photo" button: 1 License plate, 2 VIN plate, 3 Damage close-up, 4 The whole vehicle (map to slots `plate`, `vin`, `damage`, `wide`). "Take photo" opens a full-screen live camera view (getUserMedia, rear camera) with the slot's guide overlay and hint, a big round shutter button, and after capture a still preview with "Retake (N left)" and "Use photo". While uploading/reading show "Checking…" on the card; then a thumbnail and "Looks good" (green) if the reading is `read`, or "We couldn't read this. Retake it?" (amber) if `unchecked` — the claimant may still continue. The session code is drawn into the bottom corner of each captured frame on the canvas before upload. No file input anywhere. Continue enabled only when all four have a photo.
5. **Check** (`capture-06`): a green checklist of what was checked so far (one line per photo: "License plate looks good" / amber line if unchecked; "4 of 4 required photos taken live"), then review cards for each earlier step with an "Edit" link that jumps back to that step, and the four thumbnails.
6. **Sign and send** (`capture-07`): fraud warning block (light blue background, left border) with "Any person who knowingly presents a false or fraudulent claim for the payment of a loss is guilty of a crime and may be subject to fines and confinement in state prison." and small "State fraud warning (demo wording)"; full name (prefilled from the claim); checkbox "I confirm the information in this claim is true to the best of my knowledge."; a signature pad (canvas, finger or mouse, "Clear" button); "Send my claim" enabled only when name, checkbox and a non-empty signature exist.
